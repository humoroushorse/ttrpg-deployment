To access your applications, wait for the LoadBalancer to be assigned an external IP:

  kubectl get service ingress-nginx-controller -n {{ include "ingress.namespace" . }}

Once the EXTERNAL-IP is available, you can access:

  Auth Service:              http://<EXTERNAL-IP>/ttrpg/auth/health
  D&D UI:                    http://<EXTERNAL-IP>/ttrpg/dnd/
  D&D API:                   http://<EXTERNAL-IP>/ttrpg/dnd/api/v1/
  Sprint Management UI:      http://<EXTERNAL-IP>/ttrpg/sprint-management/
  Sprint Management API:     http://<EXTERNAL-IP>/ttrpg/sprint-management/api/v1/

For local development with k3d, the LoadBalancer should be available at localhost:8080

Useful Commands:

  # Check ingress controller status
  kubectl get pods -n {{ include "ingress.namespace" . }} -l app.kubernetes.io/component=ingress-controller

  # View controller logs
  kubectl logs -n {{ include "ingress.namespace" . }} deployment/ingress-nginx-controller -f

  # Check ingress resources
  kubectl get ingress -n {{ include "ingress.namespace" . }}

  # Describe ingress for routing details
  kubectl describe ingress ttrpg-ingress -n {{ include "ingress.namespace" . }}
